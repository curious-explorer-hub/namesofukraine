## Three days on the road to gold

When Russia launched its full-scale invasion, Yaroslava Mahuchikh was at home in Dnipro. The World Indoor Championships in Belgrade were only weeks away. She went by car: three days and about 2,000 kilometres through Moldova and Romania to Serbia. In Belgrade Mahuchikh cleared 2.02 m and became world champion.

## The girl from Dnipro

Yaroslava Mahuchikh was born in 2001 in Dnipro. In 2019 she won the European junior title, and at the senior World Championships in Doha she came second with 2.04 m, a world record for athletes under 20. At the Tokyo Olympics in 2021 she won bronze.

## 2.10 metres

On 7 July 2024, at a Diamond League meeting in Paris, Mahuchikh cleared 2.10 m and broke Stefka Kostadinova’s world record, set in 1987. A month later, at the Olympic Games in the same city, she became Olympic champion. She had already won the 2023 World Championships in Budapest, and in 2026 she became world indoor champion for the second time.

## The first Ultimate champion

In August 2026 in Birmingham Mahuchikh won the European title for the third time in a row. Then on 11 September 2026 in Budapest she cleared 1.99 m and became the first ever high jump champion of World Athletics’ new competition, the Ultimate Championship.

## Why it matters today

Mahuchikh wins the world’s biggest competitions for Ukraine in the years of the full-scale war. As of October 2026 the women’s high jump world record is hers.
