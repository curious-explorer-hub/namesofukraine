## Undisputed, three times over

An undisputed world champion holds the belts of all the major boxing organizations at once. Few boxers in history have done it. In July 2025 in London, Oleksandr Usyk beat Britain’s Daniel Dubois for the second time and became the first boxer to win that title for a third time.

## From Simferopol to London

Usyk was born in 1987 in Simferopol. As an amateur he became European champion (2008) and world champion (2011), and in 2012 he won the Olympic Games in London. After the Olympics he turned professional: in 2013 he signed with K2 Promotions, the company founded by the brothers [Vitalii](/en/people/vitalii-klychko/) and [Volodymyr Klychko](/en/people/volodymyr-klychko/).

## Two weight classes

Usyk started out at cruiserweight. In July 2018 he won the final of the World Boxing Super Series and became the first Ukrainian to be undisputed world champion. Then he moved up to heavyweight, where the biggest boxers fight. In May 2024 he beat Tyson Fury and united the four championship belts, and in 2025, after his second fight with Dubois, he did it again. In May 2026 in Giza, Egypt, he beat the Dutchman Rico Verhoeven and kept his unified world title.

## February 2022

When Russia launched its full-scale invasion, Usyk came back from Britain to Kyiv and joined the territorial defence. “It is my duty to fight, to defend my home, my family,” he said. The rematch with Anthony Joshua that was being planned had to wait. In 2024 the president awarded Usyk the Order of Freedom and the National Legend of Ukraine award.

## Debates and assessments

Before 2022 some Ukrainians criticized Usyk for words and actions linked to Russia. In October 2014, after Russia annexed Crimea, he took part in opening a boxing tournament in Simferopol alongside Sergei Aksyonov, whom Russia had installed as the peninsula’s leader. In 2015 he posted a photo with a St George ribbon on Instagram, and later replaced it. In a 2016 interview he said: “I don’t divide our peoples, because we are Slavs,” and the media reported this as a claim that Ukrainians and Russians are one people. In 2020 he was criticized for appearing in a film made by a Russian monastery.

Usyk himself never gave up his Ukrainian citizenship. In 2020 he answered his critics: “Don’t divide people,” and explained: “For me a Ukrainian is a Ukrainian. And it doesn’t matter whether he lives in Lviv, Crimea, Donetsk or Odesa.” After the 2022 invasion he joined the territorial defence, and in 2024 the state awarded him the Order of Freedom.

## Why it matters today

Usyk became undisputed champion in two weight classes and the first to win that title three times. And in February 2022, at the peak of his career, he came home to defend Kyiv.
