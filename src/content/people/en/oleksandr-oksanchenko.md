## Why “Grey Wolf”?

Oleksandr Oksanchenko was born on 26 April 1968 in the village of Malomykhailivka in the Dnipropetrovsk region. In 1989 he graduated from the Kharkiv Higher Military Aviation School of Pilots and became a military pilot. For more than twenty years he served with the 831st Tactical Aviation Brigade in Myrhorod, as deputy commander for flight training.

From 2013 to 2018, Oksanchenko was the brigade's display pilot: flying the Su-27, he performed aerobatic routines at Europe's biggest airshows, in Britain, the Czech Republic, Poland, Hungary, Romania, Denmark, Belgium and Malta. Audiences and fellow pilots gave him the call sign “Grey Wolf”. In 2017, at the Royal International Air Tattoo - the world's largest military airshow - he won the “As the Crow Flies” trophy for the best flying display.

## His last flight

In 2018 Oksanchenko retired from active service and became a city councillor in Myrhorod. But when the full-scale invasion began on 24 February 2022, he returned to duty. On 25 February 2022, Colonel Oleksandr Oksanchenko was killed in an air battle defending the skies over the Kyiv region. He was 53.

## How he is remembered

On 2 March 2022, Oksanchenko was posthumously made a Hero of Ukraine. Streets in Myrhorod and Kremenchuk now bear his name, and at RAF Fairford in Britain - where he once dazzled crowds - his memory is still honoured today.
