## Champion at 13

Ten metres is roughly the height of a three-storey building. In August 2019 in Kyiv, 13-year-old Oleksii Sereda dived from that height at the senior European Championships, and won. That made him the youngest European champion in the history of the event.

## The boy from Mykolaiv

Oleksii Sereda was born on 25 December 2005 in Mykolaiv. By 2021, aged 15, he was at the Tokyo Olympics, where he finished sixth twice: on the individual platform and in the synchronised event with Oleh Serbin. At the 2024 Games in Paris, Sereda was eighth on the individual platform and fifth in the synchronised event with Kyrylo Boliukh.

## Diving while there is war at home

Sereda competes for the Central Sports Club of the Armed Forces of Ukraine. His home city of Mykolaiv is under Russian shelling. In 2024 he said he wanted to help Ukraine's defenders: he was raising money for them through his posts and knew where the donations went, because his friends were at war. He said he did not understand how a terrorist state could be allowed to take part in the Olympic Games.

## Silver and a personal best

2025 brought Sereda his biggest wins. At the European Championships in Antalya he won three gold medals, including his third European title on the individual platform. Then at the World Championships in Singapore he took silver with a personal best of 515.20 points, the best result in Ukraine's history for a man in platform diving. That year European Aquatics named him Europe's best male diver.

## Why it matters today

Oleksii Sereda showed that a 13-year-old from Ukraine can beat Europe's best adult athletes. He has given Ukraine its best ever result for a man in platform diving at the World Championships, and he did it in years when his home city is under fire. For anyone training at home during the war, his path is proof that you can grow and win against the odds.
