## A bronze for two

Can two people win an Olympic medal with one dive? In synchronised diving, two athletes take off at the same moment, and every move they make has to match. At the 2008 Olympic Games in Beijing, 20-year-old Illia Kvasha and Oleksii Pryhorov dived like that from the 3-metre springboard, and won bronze.

## A boy from Mykolaiv

Illia Kvasha was born on 5 March 1988 in Mykolaiv. From 1997 he competed for the city's Ukraina sports society. As a junior he was European champion six times, and in 2008 he won the senior European title too, on the 1-metre springboard.

## King of the 1-metre board

In 2009 and 2010 Kvasha was European champion on the 1-metre board again, and won the synchronised 3-metre event as well. Three times he stood on the podium at the World Championships. At London 2012 he and Pryhorov finished fourth, and at Rio de Janeiro in 2016 Kvasha came sixth in the individual 3-metre springboard.

In 2017, at a European Championships held at home in Ukraine, he won gold on the 1-metre board once more, plus two silvers: on the 3-metre board and in synchro with Oleh Kolodii. He said then that a championship like this would make diving even more popular in Ukraine.

## A coach in wartime

After he stopped competing, Kvasha opened his own club in Ukraine and coached the national team. When Russia launched its full-scale invasion in February 2022, he stayed in Ukraine and made sure his divers moved safely to other countries in Europe. Later that year, at the World Junior Championships in Montreal, his divers made the top six. In 2023 Kvasha became head coach and program director of a diving club in Ottawa, Canada.

## Why it matters today

Kvasha brought Ukraine an Olympic medal in diving and for almost ten years won European gold for it. His path shows that Mykolaiv can lead all the way to an Olympic podium. When war came, his first thought was for the safety of his divers. For every young athlete his story is a reminder that a true champion thinks not only of his own medals but of those who come after him.
