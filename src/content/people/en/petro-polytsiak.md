## The call-up he came back for

In the summer of 2014 Petro Polytsiak's call-up papers were sent to his village council, while he was away earning a living on a building site in Kyiv. Petro came home and collected them, and by August 2014 he was in the army.

Petro was born on 5 July 1992 in the village of Borbyn in the Rivne region and went to the local school. Later he lived in Dubno and worked as a builder. In the army he became a rifleman in the 80th Separate Airmobile Brigade, with the call sign "Chornyi" - "Black".

## The new terminal

In January 2015 Petro and other soldiers of the 80th Brigade were defending the new terminal of Donetsk airport - the defenders known as "cyborgs". The troops there came under fire every day. His family recall that during that fighting Petro and a comrade carried a badly wounded soldier out under fire, for almost half a kilometre, and the comrade helping him was wounded too.

His family last heard from him on 20 January 2015; after the explosion in the terminal, there was no more news.

Private Petro Polytsiak was killed on 20 January 2015 defending the new terminal of Donetsk airport. He was 22. That same day his brigade's combat medic [Ihor Zinych](/en/people/ihor-zinych/) and [Ivan Zubkov](/en/people/ivan-zubkov/) were killed there too. Petro is buried in his home village of Borbyn.

## Why it matters today

Petro Polytsiak could have stayed on his building site in Kyiv when his call-up papers arrived at the village council. Instead he came home and collected them himself. The builder from Borbyn became one of the “cyborgs” who held the new terminal of Donetsk airport under daily fire. There, his family recall, he and a comrade carried a badly wounded soldier almost half a kilometre. Petro showed that courage means not only holding a position but also not leaving behind someone beside you who needs help. In 2021 he was posthumously made a Hero of Ukraine.
