## A student from Dnipro

Mykhailo Tkach comes from Dnipro. He studied at the Oles Honchar Dnipro National University, and that is where he realised journalism was his calling. While still a student he worked at the local Channel 9, and after graduating he moved to Kyiv to join the 1+1 TV channel.

## Schemes

In 2015 Tkach joined Schemes: Corruption in Detail, Radio Liberty’s investigative programme. Until 2021 he was its journalist and presenter, and he put out dozens of investigations into the country’s most senior officials. In 2020 Ukrainska Pravda named him journalist of the year.

## Ukrainska Pravda

Since 2021 Tkach has headed the investigations desk at Ukrainska Pravda, the outlet founded by [Georgiy Gongadze](/en/people/heorhii-honhadze/). During the full-scale war he has investigated the routes of trade with Russia, officials who went on holiday abroad while travel was restricted, and the financial scandals of Ukrainian millionaires across Europe.

## Recognition

In August 2022 Tkach was awarded the Order For Merit, 3rd class. Three times in a row he topped the Journalists of the Year ranking compiled by the Institute of Mass Information. In 2026 he received the Georgiy Gongadze Prize, which is given not for a single story but for a professional career and loyalty to the values of journalism.

## Why it matters today

Investigative journalism is one of the ways a society keeps watch over those in power. Tkach’s work shows that even in wartime, questions to officials still matter.
