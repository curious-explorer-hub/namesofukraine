## Who was Ig2000?

On 23 July 2011 a new contributor signed up to the Ukrainian Wikipedia as Ig2000. In two and a half years he wrote over 280 articles and edited over 1,600. He wrote about geography, history (above all the history of Ukraine’s armed forces), economics, football and Formula 1. The community rated his article on the destroyer Nezamozhnyk a “good article”. He sent sports news to Wikinews and ran a Facebook group for contributors to bring in new writers.

## From Zubrets to Lviv

Ig2000 was Ihor Kostenko. He was born on 31 December 1991 in the village of Zubrets in Ternopil region. He finished the St Josaphat Collegium in Buchach and in 2009 entered the Faculty of Geography at Lviv University, where he was one of the best students. He played football, supported Karpaty Lviv and, while still a student, became a journalist for the sports website Sportanalityka. With friends he planned a sports site, allsport24.com; it went live after he was gone.

## On the Maidan

From the first days of the Euromaidan, Ihor joined the student protests in Lviv that demanded the signing of the EU Association Agreement. He went to Kyiv with friends several times and was on the Maidan on the night of 30 November 2013, when riot police broke up the students’ protest. He set off for the capital for the last time with other Lviv students on 18 February 2014.

Ihor Kostenko was killed on Instytutska Street in Kyiv on 20 February 2014, as protesters moved up the street. He was 22. The historian [Bohdan Solchanyk](/en/people/bohdan-solchanyk/), a graduate of the same university, was killed on Instytutska that same morning. In 2023 the Sviatoshynskyi District Court of Kyiv found three former officers of the Berkut riot police guilty of killing protesters on the Maidan; back in 2019 they had been released from custody in a prisoner exchange with Russia.

## How he is remembered

Ihor Kostenko was posthumously made a Hero of Ukraine with the Order of the Golden Star. On 10 August 2014, at the Wikimania conference in London, Wikipedia co-founder Jimmy Wales named him Wikipedian of the Year. The Faculty of Geography opened a classroom in his name (2014) and put up a monument to him in its courtyard (2018). A school in his home village of Zubrets and a government scholarship for geography students carry his name.
