## Why “Lazybones”?

Hlib Babich was born in 1969 in Mykolaiv. He wrote poems from childhood, later began setting them to music, and with friends set up a recording studio in his home city. That was when he took the pen name “Doctor Lentiai” - Doctor Lazybones. The “Doctor” fell away, but “Lazybones” stuck, and became the call sign of a man who never stood on the sidelines.

## Six rotations and a new language

In 2014 Babich went to the front. Over five years he served six rotations - Debaltseve, Marinka, Shyrokyne, Vodiane, Avdiivka - and became an expert on the Shilka anti-aircraft guns. He commanded a platoon in the 10th Separate Mountain Assault Brigade. For most of his life Hlib had spoken and written in russian; at the front he switched to Ukrainian. His song “Podai Zbroiu” (Pass Me a Weapon) became the brigade's unofficial anthem.

## Poems, songs and brothers in arms

“Every word has to work,” Babich said of his poems. He wrote more than 500 poems and songs; seven of them are performed by the band Kozak System, among them “Molfar” and “Ne Pokyn” (Don't Leave). In 2021 his collection “Poems and Songs” came out. Babich left the army in 2019, but not the soldiers: he co-founded the volunteer initiative “Res_Publica. Brothers in Arms”, which helped Ukrainian units.

## Back in uniform

After 24 February 2022 he joined the Kyiv Territorial Defence, and later, with friends, fitted out mobile optical-electronic reconnaissance systems called “Loki” for the front. On 28 July 2022 Hlib Babich was killed near Izium in the Kharkiv region. He was 53.

## Why it matters today

Hlib Babich chose twice to defend Ukraine, and between the wars he never stopped helping those who fought. As a grown man he switched to Ukrainian, and it was in Ukrainian that his words came to life in songs soldiers sing. He showed that a poem or a song can hold a brigade together too, and that a strong word can do real work. Anyone who writes, sings or brings people together around a shared cause carries on what he did; a street in Kyiv now bears his name.
