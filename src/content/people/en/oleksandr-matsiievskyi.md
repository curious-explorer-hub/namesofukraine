## From Chișinău to Nizhyn

Oleksandr Matsiievskyi was born in 1980 in Chișinău. There he finished school and trained as an electrician. In 2008 he moved with his family to Nizhyn in Chernihiv Oblast and worked as an electrician in Nizhyn and Kyiv.

## The electrician who became a sniper

When the full-scale invasion began, Matsiievskyi volunteered. On 11 March 2022 he was enlisted in the 163rd Battalion of the 119th Separate Territorial Defense Brigade of Chernihiv Oblast. He completed his training and from November 2022 fought as a sniper in the Bakhmut sector.

In late December 2022, during the battles for Soledar, Oleksandr Matsiievskyi was taken prisoner, and on 30 December, unarmed, he was shot by Russian soldiers after he said “Slava Ukraini!”; he was 42.

## Why it matters today

Oleksandr Matsiievskyi was not a career soldier, but when the full-scale invasion began, he chose to defend the country that had become his home. He showed that Ukraine is defended by people of many paths, and that loving it does not depend on where you were born. His last words, “Slava Ukraini!”, became a symbol of resilience for Ukrainians and were heard far beyond the country; there is a monument to him even in Tbilisi. Anyone who says these words today carries on his choice.
