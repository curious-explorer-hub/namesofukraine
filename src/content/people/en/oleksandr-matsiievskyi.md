## From Chișinău to Nizhyn

Oleksandr Matsiievskyi was born in 1980 in Chișinău, where his mother had been assigned to work at a shoe factory. There he finished school and trained as an electrician. In 2008 the family moved to Nizhyn in Chernihiv Oblast, his mother's hometown. Oleksandr worked as an electrician in Nizhyn and Kyiv, and legally he remained a citizen of Moldova.

## The electrician who became a sniper

On 24 February 2022, the first day of the full-scale invasion, Matsiievskyi went to the Nizhyn enlistment office, but was not taken into the army at once. So he helped build fortifications, stood guard at checkpoints, made incendiary mixtures and kept going back to the recruiting station. On 11 March 2022 he was enlisted in the 163rd Battalion of the 119th Separate Territorial Defense Brigade of Chernihiv Oblast. He completed his training and from November 2022 fought as a sniper in the Bakhmut sector.

In late December 2022, during the battles for Soledar, Oleksandr Matsiievskyi was taken prisoner, and on 30 December, unarmed, he was shot by russian soldiers after he said “Slava Ukraini!”; he was 42. On 6 March 2023 a video of the shooting spread on social media, and the SBU later identified him.

## Why it matters today

Oleksandr Matsiievskyi was not a career soldier, but when the full-scale invasion began, he chose to defend the country that had become his home. He showed that Ukraine is defended by people of many paths, and that loving it does not depend on where you were born. His last words, “Slava Ukraini!”, became a symbol of resilience for Ukrainians and were heard far beyond the country; there is a monument to him even in Tbilisi. Anyone who says these words today carries on his choice.
