## A Hero at 21

How long does it take to become a Hero of Ukraine? For Yevhenii Hromadskyi it took eight months as an officer. He grew up in Chuhuiv in a family where both his father and mother were in the military, and he dreamed of the army from childhood. In 2017 he entered the National Academy of the National Guard of Ukraine in Kharkiv, graduated as a lieutenant in July 2021 and joined an operational brigade named after Colonel [Petro Bolbochan](/en/people/petro-bolbochan/).

## The first evening of the war

On the night of 24 February 2022, Yevhenii was the duty officer in his unit. As soon as the first missiles hit, he raised his soldiers on alert, and the platoon dug in on the approaches to Kharkiv near Hoptivka, on the direct road from Belgorod. In the evening a Russian column with Grad rocket launchers came down that road. Yevhenii and four soldiers in a Varta armored vehicle struck it from the side, and the column was destroyed.

His father, reserve colonel Oleh Hromadskyi, who had fought the Russian invaders back in 2014-2015, was fighting nearby in those hours. He had come to help with a group of volunteers that day, and he was killed in the battle for Kharkiv.

Next, Yevhenii's platoon held a line near Rohan, on the road to Chuhuiv, and beat back another attack, this time with tanks.

## After the Gold Star

On 25 March 2022 Lieutenant Hromadskyi was made a Hero of Ukraine with the Gold Star order. In May he was wounded, but after treatment he went back to service. In the summer of 2022 he moved to the 92nd Brigade, named after Otaman [Ivan Sirko](/en/people/ivan-sirko/), where he commanded a company, and later he returned to the National Guard, joining the Khartiia brigade. As of June 2025 he was a major.

## Why it matters today

Kharkiv held in the first days of the invasion thanks to people like this young lieutenant and his platoon. Yevhenii Hromadskyi showed that courage doesn't need long years of service: it takes training, a cool head and care for your soldiers. Father and son stood up for Kharkiv on the same day, and the son carried that service on. Anyone choosing a military career today can see from his example that being young is not a weakness but a strength.
