## From a factory manager back to the front

Ivan Zubkov was born on 1 November 1973 in Derazhnia, in the Khmelnytskyi region. In 1991 he entered the Lviv Higher Military-Political School, later transferring to the Kyiv Higher Naval School. He served in the armed forces until 1998, then spent sixteen years away from the military, working as a commercial director and later running his own business.

In the winter of 2013-2014 he stood on the Maidan during the Revolution of Dignity, and in August 2014, when mobilization began, he volunteered. He was assigned to the 90th Separate Airborne Battalion "Zhytomyr" of the 81st Separate Airborne Brigade, becoming deputy commander of its fire-support company. His comrades valued him for another skill too: he could repair and tune any weapon in the unit.

## Donetsk airport

In November 2014 the battalion was deployed to the Donbas. On 18 January 2015, Senior Lieutenant Zubkov took command of a fire-support platoon in assault operations meant to relieve the defenders of Donetsk airport - the men who would become known as "cyborgs," among them [Valerii Chybinieiev](/en/people/valerii-chybinieiev/) and [Ihor Branovytskyi](/en/people/ihor-branovytskyi/). He was wounded in the hand the next day but stayed in the fight.

On 20 January 2015, Senior Lieutenant Ivan Zubkov was killed under the collapsed second floor of the airport's new terminal while covering his unit's withdrawal. He was 41.

## How he is remembered

On 9 June 2015, Ivan Zubkov was posthumously made a Hero of Ukraine with the Order of the Gold Star. That same year, the 90th Separate Airborne Battalion he had served in was renamed in his honor. In his hometown of Derazhnia, memorial plaques were placed on the schools where he had studied.
