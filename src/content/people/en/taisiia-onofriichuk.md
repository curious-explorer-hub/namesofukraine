## A seven-metre ceiling

How do you train with a hoop that flies at least nine metres into the air in a hall only seven metres high? That is how Taisiia Onofriichuk prepared for the 2024 Olympics: often without electricity, and only now and then on the floor of the Palace of Sports. The attacks made it hard to concentrate on the music, which rhythmic gymnastics cannot do without, so the team moved to train where there were fewer of them. The 16-year-old received her Olympic uniform on 8 July, the day a russian missile hit Kyiv's main children's hospital.

## The Kyiv school

Taisiia Onofriichuk was born in Kyiv on 26 May 2008. She fell in love with the sport as a little girl after watching a competition, and started training at seven. Her coach is Iryna Deriugina, president of the Ukrainian Gymnastics Federation. In 2023 Onofriichuk won silver in clubs at the Junior World Championships, and at her first senior European Championships, in 2024, bronze in hoop.

## A moonwalk in Paris

At the Paris Olympics she performed her hoop routine to Michael Jackson's "Thriller": she moonwalked, copied moves from the video and caught the hoop with her feet to a roar from the stands. She was fourth in qualification and ninth in the all-around final.

## The first in 28 years

In June 2025 in Tallinn, 17-year-old Onofriichuk won the European all-around title, the first Ukrainian to do so since Olena Vitrychenko in 1997. Since the start of that season she had competed at six tournaments and reached the podium 29 times out of a possible 30. Later that year, at the World Championships in Rio de Janeiro, she won two bronze medals.

## Gold after 13 years

In August 2026 in Frankfurt am Main, Onofriichuk again took the floor to "Thriller" and scored 30.700, the best mark in the hoop final. The last time a Ukrainian had become an individual world champion was in 2013, at the championships in Ukraine, when Hanna Rizatdinova also won with the hoop. The same day Onofriichuk added silver in clubs, just 0.1 behind the winner.

## Why it matters today

Onofriichuk proved that you can reach the world's top while training through air-raid alerts, in a hall without room for a full throw. She carried on from Olena Vitrychenko and Hanna Rizatdinova and brought world gold back to Ukrainian rhythmic gymnastics. Each of her performances at major championships reminds the world that Ukraine, at war, still raises champions. For girls training in Kyiv's halls today, she is proof that poor conditions do not cancel a dream.
