## Who he was

Igor Sikorsky was born in Kyiv into the family of a professor of psychiatry. He studied at the Kyiv Polytechnic Institute and was already building his own airplanes and helicopters as a young man. In 1913 he created the Russky Vityaz, one of the world’s first multi-engine heavy aircraft, and later the Ilya Muromets, which in the First World War became the world’s first four-engine bomber.

## His story

After the revolution of 1917 Sikorsky emigrated: in 1918 to France, and in 1919 to the United States. There, in 1923, he founded an aircraft company and started almost from scratch. His S-42 flying boats opened passenger air routes across the Pacific and Atlantic oceans.

But Sikorsky’s greatest dream was the helicopter. In 1939 he flew the VS-300, the prototype of modern helicopters with a single main rotor and a tail rotor. His R-4, which first flew in 1942, became the world’s first mass-produced helicopter. Sikorsky helicopters still save people around the world today.

## Why it matters today

Sikorsky is an example of an engineer who did not stop, even after losing everything. The Kyiv Polytechnic Institute and Kyiv’s Zhuliany airport bear his name.
