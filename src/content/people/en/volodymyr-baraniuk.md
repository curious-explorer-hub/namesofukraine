## The first time they caught him

In March 2014, as Russia seized Crimea, Major Volodymyr Baraniuk was deputy commander of a marine battalion in Feodosia. When Russian troops surrounded the base and flew the senior officers out by helicopter, Baraniuk took charge of the encircled unit. Russian troops fired on the unarmed marines and threw flash grenades at them; Baraniuk came away with broken ribs and a head injury. He was released on 26 March 2014, along with the other captured marines.

## Mariupol

Eight years later, now a colonel, Baraniuk had commanded the 36th Separate Marine Brigade since September 2021. When the full-scale invasion began on 24 February 2022, his brigade found itself encircled in Mariupol and held off a far larger force for weeks. On 19 March 2022 he was named a Hero of Ukraine.

In early April 2022 Baraniuk led a column trying to break out of the besieged city. The breakout failed, and he was taken prisoner. Command of what remained of the brigade passed to [Serhii Volynskyi](/en/people/serhii-volynskyi/), who led roughly 500 soldiers through to the Azovstal plant.

## Captivity since

On 8 May 2022, Russian state media showed Baraniuk alive in captivity. As of October 2026, he remains held in Russia; no official report of his release has been made.
