## Four out of four

Imagine a World Championships where you line up again and again, and every time you cross the line first. In Milan in 2025 Liudmyla Luzan won the women's C-1 500 m and C-1 200 m, and with Iryna Fedoriv the C-2 500 m and C-2 200 m. Four golds at one championships were a personal best, and she didn't lose a single race there. After Milan she had eight World Championship gold medals.

## From Ivano-Frankivsk to the Poltava region

Liudmyla Luzan was born on 27 March 1997 in Ivano-Frankivsk. To train, she moved to Horishni Plavni in the Poltava region, which has a base with everything needed for elite sport and is home to coach Mykola Matsapura. In 2014 Luzan won silver in the canoe single at the Youth Olympic Games.

## Three Olympic medals

At the Tokyo Olympics, held in 2021, Luzan set an Olympic record in her C-1 200 m heat and went on to win bronze in the final. In the C-2 500 m she and Anastasiia Chetverikova took silver. Three years later in Paris the same crew, with Anastasiia now competing as Rybachok, won silver over that distance again. In both Tokyo and Paris, Liudmyla Luzan carried Ukraine's flag at the closing ceremony.

At the 2026 World Championships in Poznań, Poland, Luzan and Rybachok finished second in the C-2 500 m, and the next day Liudmyla defended her world title in the C-1 200 m. At the C-2 medal ceremony the Ukrainians refused to pose for a joint photo on the podium because Belarusian athletes competing under a neutral flag were there.

## Why it matters today

Liudmyla Luzan has brought Ukraine medals from two Olympics in a row and has been winning world titles for years, so the blue and yellow flag on canoeing podiums has become a familiar sight. Her wins during the war remind the world of Ukraine from the top step. Her road from Ivano-Frankivsk to Horishni Plavni shows that a goal is worth finding the right place and the right coach for. For anyone training in Ukraine today, she is an example of how to stay at the very top, year after year.
