## Who he was

Maksym Yalovtsov was born in 1990. According to the Memorial platform, which records Ukraine’s war dead, he was born in Russia, and his family moved to Donetsk Oblast when he was one; the sources available do not name the town where he was born. He spent his childhood in Torez in Donetsk Oblast (now Chystiakove), and in 2002 his family moved to Kyiv. As a boy he played football and supported Dynamo Kyiv; later he took up rugby and played for the first team of the Aviator club in the top division of the Ukrainian championship.

He also did very well in combat sports: he became a Master of Sport of Ukraine in grappling, free fight and mixed martial arts, and won medals at World and European championships in pankration and Brazilian jiu-jitsu. Maksym founded the Civil Safety (Tsyvilna bezpeka) organization, whose project, the Civil Safety Academy, taught civilians to handle firearms safely; he himself taught grappling there.

## His story

After the full-scale invasion began, Maksym and like-minded friends, many of them Dynamo supporters, gathered groups of volunteers, and from the first days he defended Kyiv in the Mriia volunteer territorial defense unit. On 20 March 2022 a Russian strike destroyed the Retroville shopping center, where he and most of his comrades were based; Maksym was the first to rush in to rescue his friends from the rubble.

Later, Private Maksym Yalovtsov, call sign “Rehbist” (“Rugbyist”), carried out combat missions deep behind enemy lines. Sources differ on his service: the decree awarding him lists him among members of the National Guard of Ukraine, while the Memorial platform describes him as a soldier of the Defense Intelligence of Ukraine (HUR). On 21 September 2022 he was killed on a combat mission on the Zaporizhzhia front. He was 32. On 18 October 2022 he was buried on the Alley of Glory at Lisove Cemetery in Kyiv. By a presidential decree of 15 February 2023 Maksym Yalovtsov was posthumously awarded the Order for Courage, 3rd class; he was also posthumously given the Kyiv mayor’s badge “For Assistance in the Defense of Kyiv”.

## Why it matters today

For the sports community and Dynamo fans, Maksym Yalovtsov became the image of an athlete who went to defend his country without hesitation. In September 2023 a mural in his honor appeared on Myloslavska Street in Kyiv’s Troieshchyna district, and his story is kept by the Sports Committee of Ukraine’s “Angels of Sport” project.
