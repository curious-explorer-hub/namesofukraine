## From the pastry kitchen to the barricades

Serhii Tabala was training to be a cook and pastry chef when the Maidan protests began in Kyiv in the autumn of 2013. He was 17, and he went there from the very first days.

Serhii was born on 16 December 1995 in Lebedyn, in the Sumy region, and went to school in Sumy. After nine years at School No. 9 he enrolled at a vocational college in Sumy to train as a cook and pastry chef. On 29 November 2013, on Kyiv's Independence Square, he joined "Right Sector", later led one of its squads, and stayed on the barricades of the Revolution of Dignity through the days of February 2014.

## "Sievier" at the airport

In summer 2014 Serhii went through training at a "Right Sector" camp and headed east as a volunteer of the 5th Battalion of the Ukrainian Volunteer Corps. His call sign was "Sievier" - "North". On 28 August he was wounded during an assault on the positions at Donetsk airport; he did not want to leave his comrades and went for treatment only on his commander's order. Once he had recovered, he went back to the airport. Another volunteer from the same battalion, [Sviatoslav Horbenko](/en/people/sviatoslav-horbenko/), also fought there.

Serhii Tabala was killed on 6 November 2014 defending the control tower of Donetsk airport. He was 18.

## How he is remembered

As early as 17 December 2014, Sumy City Council posthumously made Serhii Tabala an honorary citizen; he is buried on the alley of honorary citizens at the city's central cemetery. Memorial plaques were placed on School No. 9 and on the building where he lived, and in 2016 a street in Sumy was renamed Serhii Tabala (Sievier) Street. On 21 November 2016 he was posthumously made a Hero of Ukraine with the Order of the Gold Star.
