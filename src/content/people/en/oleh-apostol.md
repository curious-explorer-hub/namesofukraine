## From battalion to branch commander

How do you go from commanding a battalion to commanding an entire branch of the armed forces in three years? When the full-scale invasion began, Oleh Apostol led a battalion. Since June 2025 he has been commander of Ukraine’s Air Assault Forces.

## From Frankivsk to the paratroopers

Oleh Apostol was born on 17 April 1987 in Ivano-Frankivsk. In 2008 he graduated from the Petro Sahaidachnyi National Army Academy in Lviv and has served in the air assault troops ever since.

From 2014 Apostol fought in eastern Ukraine. Again and again he stormed the occupiers’ fortified positions and drew enemy fire onto himself so that his comrades could find and destroy the enemy’s guns. That same year he received his first Order of Bohdan Khmelnytskyi. Later he commanded an air assault battalion of the 80th Brigade.

## Kherson, Voznesensk, Popasna

In the first days of the full-scale war, on 26–27 February 2022, Apostol led an air assault company out of encircled Kherson without losing its people or equipment. In March he commanded the defense of Voznesensk in the Mykolaiv region. A Russian brigade stormed the town twice, but Voznesensk held, and the enemy could not encircle Mykolaiv. After that, about a dozen towns and villages were freed under his command.

In June 2022 Apostol helped hold back a Russian breakthrough from Popasna in the Luhansk region and kept it from reaching Bakhmut and Soledar.

## Commander of the 95th

In November 2022 Apostol took command of the 95th Separate Air Assault Brigade. Its units defended and attacked in the Donetsk, Kharkiv and Luhansk regions. On 18 February 2024, while the brigade was fighting on the Kupiansk front, Colonel Apostol was made a Hero of Ukraine with the Gold Star order. Later that year he took part in the offensive in Russia’s Kursk region.

## General

In November 2024 Apostol was appointed Deputy Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces, and in June 2025 commander of the Air Assault Forces. In 2026 he became a major general. As of June 2026 he commands the paratroopers and personally hands out awards to those who distinguished themselves in assaults.

## Why it matters today

In the first days of the invasion Apostol got his company out of encircled Kherson, and the defense of Voznesensk kept the enemy from encircling Mykolaiv. His rise in a few years from battalion commander to the head of a whole branch shows how this war brings forward commanders who have proved themselves in battle. Today he is responsible for the paratroopers who defend Ukrainian land every day. For anyone who chooses to serve, his story shows that responsibility for others starts with bringing your people out and holding your line.
