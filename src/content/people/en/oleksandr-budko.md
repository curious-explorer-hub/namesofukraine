## A barista who went to the front

Oleksandr Budko comes from Rivne. Before the full-scale war he studied graphic design, took his first freelance jobs and worked as a barista in a Kyiv café. On 24 February 2022 he went to the enlistment office. At first he was assigned to the IT troops, but he wanted to go to the front and volunteered for the 49th Separate Rifle Battalion Carpathian Sich. There Oleksandr, known by the call sign “Teren”, became a platoon commander.

## New legs in three months

In August 2022, in the fighting in the Kharkiv region, Oleksandr was badly wounded and lost both legs. He went through rehabilitation and got his prostheses in the United States. About three months after he was wounded, he was standing on prosthetic legs. Later he began visiting wounded soldiers in hospital, cheering them up and advising them on how to choose a prosthesis or get help from the state.

## Medals in the pool

In September 2023, at the Invictus Games in Düsseldorf, Oleksandr won bronze in the 50-metre freestyle. He also competed for Ukraine at the 2025 Games in Vancouver and Whistler. In January 2026 the President awarded him the Order For Merit, 3rd class, as a silver and bronze medallist in swimming at the Invictus Games.

## A book and a TV show

In the autumn of 2023 Oleksandr presented his autobiographical book The Story of a Stubborn Man, about his life after 24 February 2022. In 2024 he became the lead of season 13 of The Bachelor on the STB channel. As he told the American broadcaster NPR, “I wanted to show the possibilities. I wanted to give people faith.”

## Why it matters today

The war has left tens of thousands of Ukrainians without an arm or a leg. Oleksandr shows them, and everyone else, that a wound doesn’t cancel sport, creative work or ordinary life. He also campaigns for a barrier-free Ukraine, where living with a disability is no harder than living without one.
