## Who first played Hamlet in Ukrainian?

In 1943, on the stage of the Lviv Opera Theater, Shakespeare’s Hamlet was performed in Ukrainian for the first time. [Yosyp Hirniak](/en/people/yosyp-hirniak/) directed it, and the Prince of Denmark was played by Volodymyr Blavatskyi. He was born on 15 November 1900 in Galicia, in Kosiv (some sources say Kolomyia), and his real surname was Trach. He first went on stage in 1919, at the Ukrainska Besida theater.

## Theater for the suburbs

In 1926 Blavatskyi took charge of the Independent People’s Theater in Lviv. The company played not only in the city center but toured the working-class suburbs, Zamarstyniv, Pidzamche, Levandivka, with Ukrainian classics such as Natalka Poltavka.

## A year with Kurbas

In 1927 Blavatskyi went to Kharkiv and joined [Les Kurbas’s](/en/people/les-kurbas/) Berezil, a theater searching for a new, modern language of the stage. He stayed only a year, but took the Kurbas school back with him to Galicia. On his return he championed the Berezil’s experimental style at the touring Tobilevych Theater, and he later wrote memoirs about working with Kurbas.

## Zahrava in Przemyśl

In 1933, in Przemyśl, Blavatskyi founded the Zahrava theater, considered the leading theater of Western Ukraine between the two world wars. There he carried on the Berezil’s experiments: Vasyl Stefanyk’s The Land had a chorus on stage, and Bohdan Lepkyi’s Baturyn, with Blavatskyi as Mazepa, had sweeping, choreographed crowd scenes. He also staged [Mykola Kulish’s](/en/people/mykola-kulish/) The People’s Malakhii, playing Malakhii himself, and played Judas in [Lesya Ukrainka’s](/en/people/lesya-ukrainka/) On the Field of Blood.

## The Opera Theater in wartime

In 1938 Zahrava merged with the Tobilevych Theater, and after Soviet rule arrived in 1939 the company went through a crisis under political pressure. During the German occupation Blavatskyi and Hirniak led the drama company of the Lviv Opera Theater, one of the strongest theaters in Ukraine at the time. It was there that the first Ukrainian Hamlet was staged.

## Theater in a refugee camp

In 1944 Blavatskyi left for the West. In a displaced persons camp in Augsburg, in former army barracks, he founded the Ensemble of Ukrainian Actors and led it until 1949. Refugees watched plays by Franko, Lesya Ukrainka, Kulish and Stefanyk alongside world classics. In 1949 Blavatskyi moved to Philadelphia, where he staged plays for the Ukrainian community. He died there on 8 January 1953.

## Why it matters today

Blavatskyi carried the bold experiments of the Berezil from Kharkiv to Galicia, so the Kurbas school lived on even after it was destroyed in the USSR. He was the first to give Hamlet a Ukrainian voice, showing that our stage could speak the language of world classics. And in a refugee camp, among people who had lost their homes, he made theater a place where the Ukrainian language and culture stayed alive. Anyone who takes what a teacher gave them and carries it further, wherever life takes them, continues his work.
