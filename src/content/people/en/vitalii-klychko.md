## Who he is

Vitali Klitschko (Vitalii Klychko in Ukrainian transliteration) was born in 1971 in the village of Belovodskoye in Kyrgyzstan, then part of the Soviet Union, where his father, a Soviet military officer, was serving. The family moved often from garrison to garrison and in the mid-1980s settled in Ukraine, in Kyiv. Vitali graduated from the Pereiaslav-Khmelnytskyi Pedagogical Institute (1995) and earned a PhD-level degree (candidate of sciences) in physical education and sport. His younger brother is [Wladimir Klitschko](/en/people/volodymyr-klychko/), also a world boxing champion.

## His story

Klitschko first made his name in kickboxing, winning world titles many times as an amateur and as a professional. In boxing he became Ukrainian champion several times, won the first World Military Games (1995), and in 1996 turned professional. In 1999 he won the WBO heavyweight world title, and in 2004 the WBC title. In 2005 he retired because of injuries, but in 2008 he returned to the ring and won the WBC belt again, holding it until the end of 2013. In all he had 47 professional fights and won 45 of them, 41 by knockout. In 2004 he was made a Hero of Ukraine. With his brother he founded the promotion company K2 Promotions and the Klitschko Brothers Foundation, a charity.

In December 2013 Klitschko gave up his title to concentrate on politics. Back in 2010 he had become leader of the UDAR party (the name means "punch"); in 2012–2014 he was a member of the Verkhovna Rada, Ukraine's parliament, and led his party's faction there. In May 2014 he was elected mayor of Kyiv; he was re-elected in 2015 and 2020 and, as of October 2026, holds the office.

## Why it matters today

Vitali Klitschko was one of the most successful heavyweight boxers of his time. Together with his brother he made Ukrainian boxers known all over the world.
