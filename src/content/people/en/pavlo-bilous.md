## Scout, sotnyk, cheesemaker

What do a Plast scout leader, a Maidan company commander and a cheesemaker from outside Kyiv have in common? They are one man: Pavlo Bilous.

He was born in 1970 in Kamianets-Podilskyi and graduated there from the university's physics and mathematics faculty. For more than twenty years Pavlo helped build up Plast, the Ukrainian scouting movement, in regions across Ukraine.

## The Maidan and the first war

During the Revolution of Dignity, Pavlo was a sotnyk on the Maidan: he led one of the "hundreds", the self-defence companies that protected the protesters. When war broke out in the east, he went as a volunteer and fought in the Kyivska Rus and Harpun battalions. In July 2015 he was wounded near Avdiivka.

After he recovered, Pavlo settled in the village of Semypolky outside Kyiv and set up a cheese dairy there, Cheesegard.

## The battle in the Kyiv region

On 24 February 2022, when russia launched its full-scale invasion, Pavlo went back to the fight. In late March, in the Kyiv region, Master Sergeant Bilous of the Ground Forces and Senior Sergeant Oleksandr Kliuchka took on a column of enemy armour. Thanks to them, russian tanks and airborne and infantry fighting vehicles were destroyed.

On 7 April 2022 Pavlo Bilous was made a Hero of Ukraine with the Gold Star order, for personal courage and heroism in defending Ukraine. The President later presented him with the order.

## Why it matters today

The Battle of Kyiv was won not only by career soldiers but also by people who had peaceful lives and chose to leave them. Pavlo Bilous raised young people in scouting, stood on the Maidan, built his own business after being wounded, and still came back when his country needed him again. His story shows that freedom rests on people who make that choice again and again. Anyone who teaches children, builds something of their own or stands up for what is right becomes part of that defence too.
