## A war that began on his birthday

For Kostiantyn Mrochko the war began on 9 April 2014, his birthday: he turned 30 that day. He was born in 1984 in the village of Zhovtneve near Novovolynsk in the Volyn region (now Blahodatne). In 2003 he did his military service in the tank troops, and in 2009 he graduated from the National University of Food Technologies in Kyiv.

## A tank man

In the spring of 2014 Kostiantyn was among the first to go and defend his country in the east, serving as a tank gunner. He fought near Marinka, Mariupol and Ilovaisk. In Mykola Tyshyk's tank crew he led columns out of the Debaltseve area through a minefield. Later he stood guard at checkpoints near Donetsk and Marinka.

## Coming back

In 2015–2019 Kostiantyn lived in Israel, worked in construction and founded an organic farm there. When Russia's full-scale invasion began on 24 February 2022, he was again among the first to defend his country. With the 14th Separate Mechanised Brigade named after Prince Roman the Great, he fought in the Kyiv region and defended the road to Kyiv. The enemy far outnumbered them, but the tank man's actions cost it heavy losses.

Kostiantyn Mrochko was killed in action near the village of Lypivka in the Kyiv region on 8 March 2022. He was 37. Four days later, on 12 March, he was posthumously made a Hero of Ukraine, the country's highest title.

## Why it matters today

Kostiantyn Mrochko chose the front twice, though he had another life: peaceful work and a business of his own far from the war. In 2014 he helped lead his comrades out of the most dangerous places in the Donbas, and in 2022 he came back to stand in the enemy's way to the capital in the first, hardest weeks of the full-scale war. He showed that living far from home doesn't cancel your duty to it. Anyone who lives far from Ukraine can remember the tank man who came back.
