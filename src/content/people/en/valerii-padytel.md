## A star that belongs to the unit

“It’s not my award. My whole detachment received the Gold Star,” Colonel Valerii Padytel says of the title Hero of Ukraine, the country’s highest. He feels awkward when people call him a hero: in his words, the award belongs to the men who were there with him, to those who were killed, and to those who are still fighting.

## From lieutenant to commander

Valerii Padytel was born in 1978 in Dunaivtsi, in the Khmelnytskyi region of western Ukraine. He became a lieutenant in 2000, graduating from the border guard academy in Khmelnytskyi. He served in border guard detachments from Zakarpattia to Kharkiv, Sumy and Donetsk, and fought in the war in the Donbas. In 2021 he took command of the Donetsk border guard detachment.

## Border guards at Azovstal

On the night of 24 February 2022 the detachment’s positions came under air and missile strikes, and near Dokuchaievsk and Hnutove Russian tank columns tried to break through the line of contact. The detachment joined the defence of Mariupol, and on 28 February the whole unit moved into the Azovstal steelworks. From there the border guards went out in groups on combat tasks set by [Denys Shleha](/en/people/denys-shleha/) and [Denys Prokopenko](/en/people/denys-prokopenko/). Padytel’s men also met the helicopters that flew in to the plant, unloading ammunition at speed and putting the wounded on board.

## The last of his unit

In May the defenders of Azovstal followed the order to save lives and left the plant. Before they went out, Padytel photographed every one of his men, signed each photo and sent them to his commanders. He himself left last, at the end of the column, and was taken prisoner by Russia. He had promised his men he would also be the last to leave captivity, but on 21 September 2022 he was exchanged among the first. He calls that his cross to bear. On 14 October 2022 he was made a Hero of Ukraine.

## Steel Border

After the exchange Padytel went back to the war and fought near Bakhmut. In January 2023 he took command of the 15th Mobile Border Guard Detachment Steel Border, one of the assault brigades that Ukraine’s Interior Ministry had begun to form.

## Why it matters today

Valerii Padytel showed that a commander answers not only for the battle but for every person he leads, from a signed photo to a promise to be the last one out. Border guards who had watched over the frontier before the war stood alongside the National Guard and the Armed Forces in Mariupol and held on until they were ordered to leave. His words about the Gold Star are a reminder that behind every hero stands a unit. Anyone who chooses to serve today can take the main thing from his story: courage and loyalty to your own people.
