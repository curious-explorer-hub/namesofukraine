How do you take back in a few days towns the enemy has held for half a year? On 6 September 2022 Ukraine’s Defence Forces went on the offensive in the Kharkiv region without warning. On 8 September the Ukrainian flag flew over Balakliia again, and by 10 September Ukrainian soldiers were in Izium and Kupiansk. The russians retreated in such haste that they left ammunition and equipment behind. In all, the offensive freed more than 500 towns and villages and over 10,000 square kilometres of Ukrainian land.

## Why it happened

In the first days of the full-scale war russia expected to take Kharkiv quickly. The city held: its defenders kept it from being surrounded, and in May 2022 they drove the enemy back from its outskirts. American analysts wrote at the time that Ukraine appeared to have won the battle of Kharkiv.

But in the south-east of the region the russians still held Balakliia, Izium and Kupiansk. Through Kupiansk, a major rail hub, and Izium ran the routes russia used to supply its troops in the Donbas. Over the summer Ukraine spoke openly about an offensive in the Kherson region, and at the end of August announced it. To hold the south, russia moved some of its best forces there, and its defences in the Kharkiv region grew thin.

## What happened

The offensive was led by General Oleksandr Syrskyi. Tank, mechanized and air assault brigades broke through the defences, and mobile groups pushed on deep behind them so the enemy had no time to dig in. On the main line of attack advanced the 92nd Mechanized Brigade under [Pavlo Fedosenko](/en/people/pavlo-fedosenko/): it reached Shevchenkove and Kupiansk. Tactical Group “Adam” under [Yevhen Mezhevikin](/en/people/yevhen-mezhevikin/) also took part in the offensive.

On 11–13 September soldiers reached the state border in the north of the region, and the whole Kharkiv region up to the Oskil River was freed. On 12 September Ukrainian troops also entered Sviatohirsk in the Donetsk region. Then they crossed the Oskil, and on 1 October freed Lyman. Analysts called the liberation of Izium Ukraine’s greatest military success since its victory in the battle of Kyiv.

## What was found in Izium

Liberation also revealed what had happened under occupation. In a forest near Izium a mass burial site was found. Over a week, 447 bodies were exhumed there; most were civilians, women and children among them. One of the dead was the children’s writer Volodymyr Vakulenko. In Balakliia, Izium and other freed towns investigators found places where the occupiers had held and tortured people.

## Why it matters today

The Kharkiv counteroffensive showed that Ukraine could not only hold out but take its land back. The victory came not from numbers but from preparation, cunning and speed. For the people of Balakliia, Izium and Kupiansk it meant the end of occupation, and for the whole country it brought hope in the hardest year. And what was found in Izium is a reminder of why liberation cannot wait.
