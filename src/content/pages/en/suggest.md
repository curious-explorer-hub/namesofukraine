title: Suggest a person

---

Who else belongs in the catalogue? Give us the name, a few sentences on why this person matters, and sources if you have them. You can also use this form to report a mistake.

We read every suggestion, but not everyone is added: a person has to meet our [selection criteria](/en/about/), and the facts have to be backed by reliable sources. New people are added in batches - you can see them on the [New people](/en/new/) page.
