## Our texts

The profiles and page texts are written by the «Know Your Own» team. They are available under the [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/deed.en) license: you may reuse and adapt them with a credit to «Know Your Own» and a link, sharing your version under the same license. This license doesn't cover quotations, images or videos by other authors.

## Images

Most images are in the public domain or under free licenses: they come from Wikimedia Commons and from official websites that publish under CC BY 4.0. Each image's author, license, source and any changes are shown on that person's page. Some portraits were restored, colorized or turned into photo-like images with AI; the credit says so. The images on the home page are listed below.

## Images without a free license

For some people, mostly those no longer living, we found no free photo. We then show a portrait from another source, credit its author (where known) and source, and note that it isn't covered by the CC BY-SA license. We use these images only on this site, to show who a non-commercial educational profile is about, and never in share images, social media posts or materials for our supporters. Please don't reuse them.

## Quotes and videos

Short quotations are given with their author and source, to tell the person's story. Videos play from the publisher's own YouTube channel and aren't stored on this site.

## For rights holders and families

If you hold the rights to an image or text on this site, or a photo shows someone in your family, [write to us](/en/feedback/?type=other#form): name the page and the image and, if you are the rights holder, how we can confirm it. Within 48 hours we'll remove or replace the image, or add the credit you ask for, and with your permission we can keep it.
