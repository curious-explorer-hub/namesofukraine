title: Image credits
description: Where the site's images come from and under which licenses.
---

Most of the site's images come from Wikimedia Commons and other open sources and are in the public domain or under free licenses. For some people no free photo exists; we then use an image under fair use - on this site only, for non-commercial education - and remove it at the rights holder's request ([write to us](/en/feedback/)). Some portraits were restored, colorized or turned into photo-like images with AI. Each portrait's author, license, source and any such changes are shown on that person's page.
