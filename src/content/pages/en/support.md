---
title: Support the project
description: How to help Know Your Own - with a donation, your time or your skills.
---

Know Your Own is a non-profit project run by enthusiasts. The site has no ads, no paywall and no tracking, and it never will. We find the sources, write and check every profile, translate it into English and look for the portraits ourselves.

Donations go to what the project needs: the domain, permission to use photos and, in time, commissioned portrait illustrations.
