## Applied
- **Repin** (ERROR): "after 1917 the village found itself in Finland" was wrong, because Kuokkala was already in the Grand Duchy of Finland. Now: the village was in the Grand Duchy; when Finland declared independence at the end of 1917 it ended up outside Russia (ЕІУ: «на теренах тогочасної Фінляндії»; IEU: from 1900 in Kuokkala).
- **Repin**: Oksana Semenik is described as "journalist and historian", as in CNN (the draft had "researcher").
- **Repin**: the Kharkiv Art Museum catalogue page (museum-digital, second version 1889–1896) added as a source for the Kharkiv version, which had been sourced to Wikipedia only.
- **Zerov** (ERROR): «Від Куліша до Винниченка» changed 1928 → 1929, as in ESU and ЕІУ (IEU says 1928).
- **Krymskyi**: "then taught for more than twenty years" changed to "taught at the Lazarev Institute from 1896 until his move to Kyiv" (ESU: from 1896; IEU: after 1898). Zvenyhorodka "(today the Cherkasy region)" added (ESU).
- **Dziuba**: "books on Khvylovyi" changed to "a book" (ESU and IEU list one, 2005).
- **Briukhovetskyi**: IEU wording made exact: "generally considered the least corrupt of the country's classical universities" (the draft had "one of the least corrupt in Ukraine"). Vladikavkaz coordinates 43.017/44.65 → 43.04/44.678 (Wikipedia page).

The Met's quote and Matiossian's argument match the sources. The Debates section is neutral, and the summary states the tie precisely (born in Chuhuiv; "worldwide mostly called a Russian painter").

- Zerov: 26.04.1890 – 03.11.1937, Sandarmokh (ESU, IEU, ЕІУ). Arrest on the night of 27–28.04.1935 at Pushkino near Moscow, sentence 1–4.02.1936 (10 years), troika 9.10.1937, rehabilitated 31.03.1958 (ESU). "Ad fontes!" and the 24.05.1925 debate (ESU).
- Dziuba: ESU says expelled 2.03.1972, arrested 18.04.1972, tried 12–16.03.1973, pardoned 6.11.1973. IEU says arrested January 1972, expelled April 1972, sentenced April 1973, released November 1973. The text gives years only, plus "November 1973" for the release, on which both agree. Babyn Yar speech (IEU), the 1965 protest with Stus and Chornovil (Radio Svoboda, Korohodskyi), Moroz and Pliushch (IEU). «Грані кристала» has no year in the text (ESU 1976, IEU 1978). The image's CC BY-SA 3.0 rests on the Commons uploader's statement about ЕІУ images.
- Briukhovetskyi: alive. No death report found (searches; the NaUKMA library page has data to 1.01.2026). Born 14.07.1947 in Vladikavkaz (ESU), which was named Dzaudzhikau then; the family moved to Cherkasy two weeks later (Україна молода interview). Hero of Ukraine by decree 559/2007 of 25.06.2007; the quoted wording matches the decree. Honorary president since September 2007 (NaUKMA library).
