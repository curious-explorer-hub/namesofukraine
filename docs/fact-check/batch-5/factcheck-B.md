## Applied
- **Vynnychenko** (ERROR): "finished the Yelysavethrad gymnasium" changed to "studied at" (ESU: «навч.» 1890–99; the gymnasium he finished is not named in any source I opened).
- **Vynnychenko**: the Solukha sentence now rests only on what we can see, the book's title as given in the IEU bibliography ("Treaty with Moscow against Hetman Pavlo Skoropadskyi: … to Moscow, to Lenin, for help"). Nobody opened the book, so the draft's paraphrase of its argument was removed.
- **Vyshyvanyi** (ERROR, attribution): the «майбутній монарх» quote is the historian Olesia Isaiuk on Radio Svoboda, talking about the Habsburg family's plans. The draft put it in the mouths of "some Ukrainians in 1918". It is now attributed to her. The 1918 hetman candidacy is now sourced to ESU. "He never gave up hope of a throne" now cites LIGA.net's summary of Snyder's book.
- **Vyshyvanyi** (ERROR, attribution): the post-war contacts with nationalists came from Kirkus (Snyder) and from Radio Svoboda's own text, not from a Snyder quote. Fixed. Added, from Radio Svoboda: his own claim under interrogation that he worked with British intelligence against Hitler, and RS's note that the topic needs more research (for balance).
- **Dzhemilev**: "15 years in prison" now reads "15 years in camps, prisons and exile". ЕІУ gives 15 years. KHPG's sentences add up to about 11 years of camp plus 4 years of exile in Yakutia. Slovo i Dilo says 15. The hunger strike now falls "during the investigation and trial in Omsk" (KHPG: from June 1975 to the verdict of 15 April 1976). Added decree 749/2023 (zakon.rada.gov.ua) as a source. Its text names him «лідеру кримськотатарського народу, правозахиснику, народному депутату України», and the motive matches the quote in the profile.
- **Sentsov**: military service brought up to date. He joined Kyiv's Territorial Defense (Interfax), was commissioned as an officer in 2023 and commanded an assault company of the 47th Brigade (Interfax, Kyiv Post), and became a battalion commander in **September 2025** (Interfax-Ukraine, 13.09.2025). The text says plainly that this is the latest information as of October 2026. Added the 7 Sep 2019 exchange of 35 for 35 (PEN International). en role "former Kremlin prisoner" changed to "former political prisoner", to match uk.
- **Yalovtsov** (ERROR): "sources differ on his birthplace" is not supported. Both sources that mention it (Memorial, uk.wiki) say Russia without naming a town, and Memorial adds that the family moved to Donetsk Oblast when he was one. No opened source has the Kyiv or "mid-air" versions. The text and `birthplace.name` now say so plainly. The organization is «Цивільна безпека», and Civil Safety Academy was its project (Sports Committee, Obozrevatel).
