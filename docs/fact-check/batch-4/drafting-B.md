The owner decided to use **Sukha (Poltava region)** as the birthplace, following ESU, ЕІУ and IEU. The long bio mentions Lomivka once, neutrally: «за іншими даними - у Ломівці, нині в межах Дніпра» / "other sources give Lomivka, now part of Dnipro". The source for the alternative is the Dnipropetrovsk regional library page (https://library.dp.ua/?id=631), which is listed in `sources`. uk-Wikipedia and poltava365.com say the same.

**Sources opened**
- ESU, https://esu.com.ua/article-30828: 03.04.1918 in Sukhe (Kobeliaky raion, Poltava oblast) to 14.07.1995 in Kyiv. Also: academician 1978, Writers' Union of Ukraine (СПУ) board head 1959–71, Hero of Socialist Labour 1978, Hero of Ukraine 2005 (posthumous), front-line volunteer in the students' battalion, twice wounded, the Kholodna Hora camp, Dnipro University 1946, «Прапороносці» (Stalin Prizes), «Людина і зброя» (Shevchenko Prize 1962), «Тронка» (Lenin Prize 1964), «Собор» condemned and withdrawn for 20 years, the move away from socialist-realist canons from the 1960s, defense of the Ukrainian language, deputy of the USSR Supreme Soviet, the posthumous «Щоденники».

- IEU, Honchar, Oles: Sukha, Kobeliaky county; Writers' Union head 1959–71; «Собор» censured and removed from circulation; "most [works] closely adhere to... socialist realism"; translated into more than 40 languages.

It could be added if a non-wiki source is found. His birth name Bilychenko (from the Lomivka sources only) is also left out.
