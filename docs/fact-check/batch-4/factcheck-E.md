## Confirmed (highlights)
- Matviienko: 10.10.1947 Nedilyshche – 08.10.2023 Kyiv; People's Artist 1985, Shevchenko Prize 1988, Hero of Ukraine 2006, National Legend of Ukraine posthumously (decree 509/2024). The KNPU "deputy 1998" claim was rightly left out: she withdrew after the 1998 election and never sat in the Rada.
- Kyrychenko: life dates, Cherkasy Folk Choir 1968–83, Poltava Philharmonic 1986–94, Hero of Ukraine 2003, Baltimore honorary citizen diploma 1983 (ЕІУ).
- Yaremchuk: 30.11.1951 Rivnia (now Vyzhnytsia) – 30.06.1995 Chernivtsi; seismologist job; Smerichka from 1969; Chornobyl concerts 1986; Shevchenko Prize 1996, Hero of Ukraine 2021 (ВУЕ, KNPU, UINP). Coordinates (central Vyzhnytsia) acceptable.
- Ruslana: 24.05.1973 Lviv; Eurovision 2004 (280 points); fun fact confirmed on eurovision.com; People's Deputy 2006–07 stated neutrally; Stus Prize 2014.
