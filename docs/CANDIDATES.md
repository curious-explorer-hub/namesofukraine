## Editorial notes

- **Fallen defenders:** the families are living and grieving. Write about public service and deeds only; nothing private. Don't describe graphic details or link to execution videos; state what happened plainly. Check every claim against decrees (president.gov.ua, zakon.rada.gov.ua), unit or ministry statements, and reputable media; encyclopedias rarely cover people who died recently. The «Захисники» / "Defenders" group was created on 2026-09-30; adding these rules to §7 is still open.
- **Not recommended** (owner to confirm): Soviet leaders and party officials, Soviet propaganda figures, and current partisan politicians. Profiling them as "people who made Ukraine" would contradict the editorial policy (§7) and Ukraine's decommunization framework. Revisit only if the site adds a "history's other side" section.
- **Collectives** (Захисники Азовсталі, Невідомий солдат, Герої Небесної Сотні): skip, create a special "collective" profile type, or profile individuals?
- **Current or serving leaders:** policy §7.6 prefers lasting achievements over current positions. Add now, or after their time in office?
