**Flags:** 👩 woman · 🟢 living · ⚖️ contested (needs a «Дискусії та оцінки» section) · 🌍 born or worked mainly outside Ukraine (state the connection precisely) · 👥 more than one person in one entry

Facts in Notes are **unverified** unless a source is named: check each against the encyclopedias (ЕСУ, ЕІУ, IEU), and awards against the decree, before drafting.

## Editorial notes

- **Fallen defenders:** the families are living and grieving. Write about public service and deeds only; nothing private. Don't describe graphic details or link to execution videos; state what happened plainly. Check every claim against decrees (president.gov.ua, zakon.rada.gov.ua), unit or ministry statements, and reputable media; encyclopedias rarely cover people who died recently.
- **Not recommended** (owner to confirm): Soviet leaders and party officials, Soviet propaganda figures, and current partisan politicians. Profiling them as "people who made Ukraine" would contradict §7 and Ukraine's decommunization framework.
- **Collectives** (Захисники Азовсталі, Невідомий солдат, Герої Небесної Сотні): skip, create a "collective" profile type, or profile individuals?
- **Current or serving leaders:** §7.6 prefers lasting achievements over current positions. Add now, or after their time in office?
